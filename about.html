Our Story

◈ ◈ ◈

Welcome to Toprak Restaurant, where fresh ingredients and thoughtful cooking come together in every dish. Inspired by the Turkish word for earth, our name reflects a deep connection to nature, balance, and grounding flavours.

Each plate is prepared with care, bringing together light textures and fragrant touches that feel comforting and vibrant. Join us for a relaxed meal in our welcoming space, enjoy convenient takeaway or delivery, or let us cater your special gatherings.

However you dine, we're here to share food that feels honest, warm, and made to be enjoyed together. Every visit brings comfort, flavour, and genuine hospitality.

Meet Our Team

The passionate people behind every dish, every experience, and every warm welcome at Toprak.

AK

Ali Kaya

Founder & Head Chef

With over 20 years of culinary experience rooted in traditional Turkish cuisine, Ali brings authenticity and creativity to every dish. His philosophy centres on respecting ingredients and letting natural flavours shine through careful preparation.

EY

Elif Yilmaz

Restaurant Manager

Elif ensures every guest feels at home from the moment they walk through the door. With a background in hospitality management, she orchestrates the warm, attentive service that defines the Toprak experience.

MD

Mehmet Demir

Sous Chef & Menu Consultant

Mehmet combines classical training with a modern sensibility, designing menus that balance tradition with innovation. His expertise in spice blending and seasonal cooking adds depth and character to our offerings.
